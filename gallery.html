Demo videos

Every demo shows the same moment three times: the clean video, the attacked video, and the attacked video after correction, each with a perception model's output. White boxes match the model's output on the clean video; dashed boxes are objects it missed, purple boxes are things that are not there, and amber boxes carry the wrong label. Each demo can be downloaded as the side-by-side video and as the three plain streams.
