Video gallery

Each demonstration shows the same segment of the clean video, the attacked video and the video corrected by StrayScope, each processed by a perception model. White boxes indicate outputs that match the model's output on the clean video. Dashed boxes indicate missed objects, purple boxes indicate spurious (ghost) detections, and amber boxes indicate incorrect labels. Each demonstration can be downloaded as the side-by-side video and as the three individual streams.
